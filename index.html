How I think about robot intelligence

What are the main real-world applications of MAPF? I recommend this video from Amazon Robotics. I believe PIBT and RAILGUN have captured the essential ideas of MAPF. In that sense, I consider MAPF to be solved.

What caused the recent qualitative leap from traditional robots to intelligent robotic systems? I believe the answer is LLMs, rather than a fundamental improvement in physical form.

Cars, forklifts, excavators, trains, and airplanes have long been mechanically capable when operated by humans. If we hide the interaction between the human and the machine, the system appears autonomous. Hands are not the essence of intelligence either: even with a pair of chopsticks in each hand, humans can complete a surprisingly wide range of tasks.

Robots need well-designed motion primitives, provided by classical control, motion planning, or VLA models. The remaining challenge is intelligence: deciding how these primitives should be composed, coordinated, and adapted to the environment.

I am therefore shifting toward LLM-based algorithm design for robotic warehouses: designing and continuously specializing task assignment, routing, coordination, congestion management, and replanning algorithms.
